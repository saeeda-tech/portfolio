Machine learning and forecasting · 2026

Day-ahead electricity demand forecasting

A leakage-safe day-ahead hourly load forecasting pipeline on US EIA-930 grid data across three balancing authorities: CAISO, PJM and ERCOT.

3.5% to 4.3% MAPE using gradient boosting on lag and calendar features alone. It outperformed CAISO’s published day-ahead forecast by 39% and matched PJM’s, without weather inputs.

Data quality · November 2026

Data quality management workflow for a field survey

A ten-stage workflow for a household health survey in Kaduna State, from planning and form design to profiling, cleansing, outlier checks and release.

A decision gate sends data that misses its targets back for a root-cause fix. Eight quality dimensions are measured, and the raw data is never overwritten.
