Desk review · November 2026

Forecasting monthly malaria cases in Nigeria from climate and surveillance data

Rainfall and temperature carry advance information with lags of one to two months. The evidence does not yet show how much they improve forecasts under honest validation.

Problem

As cited in the review (WHO, 2025), there were an estimated 282 million malaria cases and 610,000 deaths worldwide in 2024, and Nigeria carries roughly 24.3% of cases and 30.3% of deaths. Transmission follows the seasons, which is what makes forecasting possible.

What I did

  • Reviewed the literature on forecasting malaria cases from climate and surveillance data.
  • Identified what the evidence supports and where it is thin.
  • Set out the gap in how forecasts are validated.

Results

  • A companion project proposal sets out a study designed to close the validation gap.

Tools

  • Literature review
  • evidence synthesis
