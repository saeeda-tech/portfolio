Machine learning and forecasting · 2026

Day-ahead electricity demand forecasting

3.5% to 4.3% MAPE using gradient boosting on lag and calendar features alone. It outperformed CAISO’s published day-ahead forecast by 39% and matched PJM’s, without weather inputs.

Problem

Grid operators need accurate day-ahead demand forecasts to plan generation and avoid both shortfalls and costly over-supply.

What I did

  • Built a leakage-safe pipeline on about 26,000 hourly observations for each of the three balancing authorities.
  • Validated it with five-fold expanding-window backtesting.
  • Used gradient boosting on lag and calendar features alone.
  • Diagnosed the failure in ERCOT as extrapolation beyond training-range demand during the record 2023 summer load, shown by a systematic forecast bias of -1.85%.

Limits

  • No weather inputs were used.
  • The ERCOT forecasts failed at record demand because the model extrapolated beyond its training range.

Tools

  • Python
  • pandas
  • NumPy
  • scikit-learn
  • gradient boosting
  • expanding-window backtesting
