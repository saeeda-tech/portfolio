Data quality management workflow for a field survey

A decision gate sends data that misses its targets back for a root-cause fix. Eight quality dimensions are measured, and the raw data is never overwritten.

Problem

Poor data enters through the collection system, the form design or direct edits to the database. A survey needs controls at every stage, not only cleaning at the end.

What I did

  • Designed ten stages: plan and set requirements, design the form, collect, profile, cleanse, transform and enhance, check outliers, measure quality, approve and release, monitor and improve.
  • Built prevention into the tablet form: dropdown lists, required questions, number limits, a unique household ID and a duplicate warning.
  • Set a quality target for each critical field and measured eight dimensions: accuracy, consistency, completeness, precision, timeliness, validity, auditability and uniqueness.
  • Added separation of duty: someone who did not clean the data signs off the release.

Results

  • A workflow diagram with a decision gate and a root-cause loop.
  • Root-cause fixes for common findings, for example blanks mean the question was not required in the form.

Limits

  • The quality targets are proposals for discussion. The course gives no fixed numbers.

Tools
