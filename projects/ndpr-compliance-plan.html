Data governance · November 2026

NDPR compliance plan for a Kaduna State MDA

Compliance is a programme, not a one-off task: structure, data mapping, lawful basis, policy, rights, security, third parties, training and audit.

Problem

A government body holds staff records, citizens’ contact details and, in some units, health information, and must meet the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

What I did

  • Built on the NDPR and NITDA’s Implementation Framework.
  • Covered the Data Protection Officer and the unit under the CEO’s office, a data inventory, a lawful basis for each activity, a privacy policy, retention periods and data subject rights.
  • Added a breach procedure with notice to NITDA within 72 hours, written contracts with third parties, staff training and an annual audit filed by 15 March.

Results

  • Penalties noted: 2% of the previous year’s annual gross revenue or ₦10 million for controllers with more than 10,000 data subjects, and 1% or ₦2 million for smaller ones.
  • A note to review the plan against the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Limits

  • Written as an assumed scenario, so it contains no real organisation’s figures.
